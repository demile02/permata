# Permata visual direction

## Design Read

Permata is a school news and internship journal. Use the visual language of a digital newsroom with a printed editorial sensibility: paper white, warm neutral rules, bold sans-serif headlines, a restrained red accent, and metadata that reads like an issue label. Keep the existing MAN logo. Prioritize reading and source context over decorative cards.

**Dials:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1

- **Energy 2:** Calm and readable, with red reserved for the identity, active navigation, and editorial accents.
- **Rhythm 3:** Vary the masthead, headline, story copy, and editorial notes so each page has a clear reading pace.
- **Motion 1:** Keep navigation and controls immediate; use only brief hover and focus transitions.

Public Sans carries the reading interface from navigation through article body, adapted from the user's Tirto reference for a familiar newsroom rhythm and clear reading hierarchy. Use 16px body text with a 24–28px line height, article headlines around 40px, section headlines between 18px and 24px, and 12–14px metadata. Scale display headlines fluidly on smaller screens. Small uppercase labels identify edition context and sections. Warm neutrals separate editorial layers without introducing extra brand colors. The school logo remains the only illustrative asset.

The featured-story gradient is reserved for photographs so headline text remains legible over the image.
